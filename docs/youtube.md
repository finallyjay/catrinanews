# YouTube

**Title**
Catrina News: today's tech news as Día de los Muertos calaveras (Gemma 3 + ElevenLabs)

**Description**
La Catrina reads you today's tech news, in verse.

Catrina News turns the day's tech headlines into calaveras literarias, the satirical Día de los Muertos poems Mexico's 19th-century press used to mock the powerful. Gemma 3, an open-weight model running on a laptop, picks the headlines and writes the verses; plain Python checks every Spanish rhyme and syllable count and tells the model what to fix; ElevenLabs gives La Catrina her voice.

🌐 https://catrinanews.tech
💻 https://github.com/finallyjay/catrinanews

Built for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge, Week 4: Ghost in the Machine (DEV + MLH).

Headlines: SerpApi (Google News) and El País. Model: Gemma 3 12B via Ollama. Voice and avatar: ElevenLabs. Hosting: Render, on a .tech domain.
